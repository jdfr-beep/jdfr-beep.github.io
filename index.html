A note from Yuki

I did not learn these methods from a cleaning aisle. They came from passed-down household knowledge, the habits I grew up around, and time spent visiting Japanese homes and villages — watching how families keep small spaces calm, clean and useful without filling cupboards with products.

Much of that knowledge is never formally explained. It is spoken across kitchen tables, learned by watching, or simply repeated because a mother or grandmother showed the next person what worked.

These books are not about making your home look staged. They are about spending less, wasting less, and building a home that asks less of you every week.
